How the radar prices a token that does not exist yet

Implied value = TVL × comp multiple × growth × maturity × breadth

  • Comp multiple. The median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of live-token protocols in the same category. When a category has fewer than 3 live comparables, the global median across all live tokens is used instead. Clamped to 0.05–1.00.
  • Growth. 1 + (7-day TVL change, clamped to −60%..+120%) ÷ 100 × 0.5. A protocol growing fast is worth more than one shrinking at the same TVL.
  • Maturity. 0.6 + min(age in days, 1095) ÷ 1095 × 0.4. Older protocols count for more; unknown age counts as 365 days.
  • Breadth. 1 + min(chains − 1, 5) × 0.02. Each extra chain, up to five, adds 2%.

The snapshot is a frozen DefiLlama pull of every protocol at or above $5M TVL, fetched 2026-11-14. The watchlist is the 40 pre-token protocols at or above the $50M floor. The board ranks every rankable pre-token protocol in the snapshot, not just the watchlist. Wrapped, staked, bridged and exchange products are excluded, as are versioned pools.
